What is Temple IEEE?

Temple IEEE is the IEEE Student Branch at Temple University—a place for students interested in engineering and technology to meet, learn, and grow together.

What we do

We create opportunities beyond the classroom through technical workshops, mentorship, speaker events, collaborative projects, networking, and career development.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions turn classroom concepts into practical skills. Members learn by building together through circuits, soldering, software, and technical projects.
